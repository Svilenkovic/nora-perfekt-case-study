## About the project

Nora Perfekt does two jobs that rarely share a website. It cleans homes, offices and spaces after renovation, and it lays Turkish cobblestone, stone cubes, pavers, curbs and natural stone in yards, on paths and on driveways. The firm is based in the Lebane municipality and takes work anywhere in Serbia. Every job starts with a call or with photos sent over Viber.

I did not want either service to look like an add-on to the other, so I gave each its own hub page and subpages, five for cleaning and eight for paving. The home page gives them the same space, and the structured data lists them as two separate catalogs. Since the firm works nationally, the pages are written for searches across all of Serbia.

## What I built

- 21 pages: a hub and detail pages for each service, plus work, about, contact and privacy pages
- Call and Viber buttons for both numbers, with a short note for phones that have no Viber installed
- A gallery of finished work with an enlarged view of each photo
- A mobile menu, and animations that stop when the system asks for reduced motion
- Automated checks of links, media and security headers before every release
- Structured data for the business, with cleaning and paving as two offer catalogs
